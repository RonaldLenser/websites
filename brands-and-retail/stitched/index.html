The Process

How Your Piece
Comes to Life

Every commission follows our four-stage atelier process.

01

Commission

Submit your commission request. We'll respond within 48 hours to schedule your design consultation — in person or virtual.

02

Design

We take your measurements, gather your preferences, and co-design your piece. You approve the spec sheet before a single cut is made.

03

Craft

Our artisans build your piece by hand. For suits: multiple fittings. For shoes and bags: material previews before final construction.

04

Deliver

Your finished piece arrives packaged as it deserves — in a STITCHED dust bag, tissue, and keepsake box. Yours. Permanently.
