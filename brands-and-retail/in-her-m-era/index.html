Meet Jerilyn

Jerilyn is a Lehi mom of two boys, a former teacher, and a breast cancer survivor.

She taught school for six years before starting In Her M Era in 2024. She learned to weld, built the business from the ground up, and kept it going through her cancer treatment. The M in the name is for her two sons.

Today she works from her studio inside Embrace Beauty Salon in Lehi, and at private appointments and events.

Questions

What is permanent jewelry?

A chain fitted to you and welded closed instead of fastened with a clasp, so you can wear it every day without taking it on and off.

How much does it cost?

Rings are $25, bracelets $65, bangles and anklets $75, and necklaces $95. Charms start at $5.

Does the weld hurt?

No. The weld is quick and most people barely feel anything.

Can I take it off?

Yes. It can be cut off with scissors whenever you need it removed.

How long does an appointment take?

Most single pieces take about 15 to 20 minutes.

What about airport security or an MRI?

It will not set off airport metal detectors. For an MRI you will need to have it removed first.

Can I bring friends?

Yes. Jerilyn does private appointments and events. Send a message on Instagram to plan one.
