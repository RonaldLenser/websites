Who We Are

Built for the roads
of today. Designed
for tomorrow.

The PLUGG EV is a community-driven EV charging network that puts accessible infrastructure where it matters most. We identify charging gaps in underserved and emerging markets and deploy smart, high-availability hubs that eliminate range anxiety for real drivers.

We don't just install chargers. We build energy corridors — connected systems that grow with the communities they serve.

Our Vision

Seamless charging
access — everywhere,
for everyone.

We see a fully electrified future where pulling up to a charger is as effortless as pumping gas used to be. Our network is being built to be interoperable, high-availability, and community-anchored — from dense urban corridors to the suburbs where EV infrastructure has historically been sparse.
