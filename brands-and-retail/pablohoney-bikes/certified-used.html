Certified Used Bikes

PabloHoney Certified Used Bikes for sale

Our Certified Used Bike program is similar to what you'll see with certified used cars. We only purchase the highest quality, race ready bikes that are in the best condition. We tune them and inspect them to make sure they meet the highest standards.

Our goal is to offer the highest quality race bikes for your kids, or incredibly high quality used bikes for anyone, at a reasonable cost that won't break the bank. Our certified used bikes are an incredible deal and our service is unmatched.

All bikes listed here are part of our certified used bike program. They all include:

  • LIMITED LIFETIME WARRANTY!! (See our warranty page)
  • Fully tuned and ready to ride. Fresh tires, sealant and brake pads
  • 10% off all parts and accessories as long as you own the bike
  • 10% off all labor on the bike as long as you own it
  • Buyback program means you get a great offer on the bike when you're finished with it.
