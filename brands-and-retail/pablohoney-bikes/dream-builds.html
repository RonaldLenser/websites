Dream Builds

Let us build your dream bike with each component chosen specifically for your riding style and needs.

dream-builds/santa-cruz-shop.jpg

We start with an in depth consultation to assess what kind of a bike you want to build. The process continues like this:

  1. Find the perfect frame
  2. Put together a parts list to match your exact riding and price needs
  3. Do a custom fit session
  4. Build that dream bike
  5. Ride ride and more ride

Custom built bikes come with a *lifetime warranty (see details on our "about us" page) and one year no labor charges. You also get 10% off all parts for 1 year after the purchase and lifetime support.
