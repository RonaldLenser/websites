PabloHoney Custom Wheels

Each of our wheels are built by hand to meticulous standards. They are light and strong and come with our best in the industry lifetime warranty. They are also totally customizable with spoke and hub upgrades of your choice.

These are our 4 main in house build options

Agnes
wheels/custom/agnes.jpg

The Agnes is our aggressive cross country race wheel. Its ridiculously light, strong and wide enough to run bigger tires for a solid footprint on any conditions.

Felix
wheels/custom/felix.jpg

The Felix is a slightly stouter version of The Agnes. Still very light with the same width but for more aggressive XC or slightly bigger riders.

Oscar
wheels/custom/oscar.jpg

The Oscar takes the idea of hard cross country riding, and adapts it to all mountain terrain with a wide 30mm inner diameter but still coming in super light yet strong enough for more assertive terrain.

Rover
wheels/custom/rover.jpg

The Rover is our "do anything" all mountain workhorse. 30mm inner width allows up to a 2.6" tire and is strong enough to huck all day.

Build time: Custom wheel builds typically take 7 to 10 working days. If any parts need to be ordered in, it can take a little longer. Your expected wait time is included with your price estimate.
