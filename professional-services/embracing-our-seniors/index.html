Our Story

About
Embracing Our Seniors

Embracing Our Seniors was founded with a simple but powerful belief: every senior and adult with a disability deserves a home — not just a facility. A place with familiar faces, consistent routines, and genuine warmth.

Located in Union, South Carolina, we provide a small, residential environment where our staff knows every resident personally. We take the time to understand each person's preferences, history, and needs — because details matter when someone you love is in our care.

We work closely with families throughout the placement process and beyond, maintaining open lines of communication so you always feel connected and confident. Our home is your loved one's home, and we are honored to welcome them.
