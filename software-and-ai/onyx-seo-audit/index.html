The Core Problem in One Paragraph

Onyx Supplements has built a premium brand with a real differentiator — clinical dosages, transparent labeling, and a luxury aesthetic that is genuinely underrepresented in the supplement market. However, the SEO infrastructure that would allow Google, AI models, and potential customers to find the brand does not yet exist. There are no meta descriptions, no Product schema (the existing schema incorrectly labels the flavor as the brand), no blog content, no customer reviews, no third-party certifications, and no named founders. As a YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) site selling an ingestible product, Google applies maximum trust scrutiny — and Onyx currently has no trust signals beyond a GMP manufacturing claim referencing an unnamed facility. This is entirely fixable, and the absence of legacy technical debt means the brand can be built correctly from the start.

Section 02
Top 5 Critical Issues
Critical
No Product Schema — Product Page Ineligible for Rich Results & Google Shopping
The Golden Hour product page has zero Product schema markup. The existing JSON-LD on the site is a WebSite type that incorrectly credits the theme developer as the site creator. The Microdata on the product page has the brand field set to "Mango Pineapple" (a flavor, not the brand name), all schema URLs use http:// instead of https://, and no aggregateRating is present. Google cannot display product rich results (price, availability, star ratings) in SERPs. The product is ineligible for Google Shopping free listings. This affects every commercial SERP appearance.
Fix: Add Product JSON-LD to the product template with correct brand name ("Onyx Supplements"), absolute HTTPS image URLs, Offer block, availability set to PreOrder, and wire Okendo review data into aggregateRating. Code provided in Section 13.
Critical
No Meta Descriptions Sitewide — Google Will Auto-Generate Poor Snippets
Every page on theonyxsupps.com — homepage, product page, about, FAQ, manifesto, contact — has no meta description set. For a luxury brand where first impressions in search results are critical, Google will pull random on-page text as the snippet. On a minimalist brand site with sparse copy, this means non-descriptive, non-converting SERP snippets. This also wastes the homepage's potential to rank for brand-adjacent queries.
Fix: Set unique 120–155 character meta descriptions in Shopify Admin for every page. Takes under 1 hour. Highest effort-to-impact ratio fix on the entire site.
Critical
Anonymous Brand — No Named Founder, No Contact Info, No Credentials (YMYL Failure)
Supplements are classified as YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) content by Google. The Quality Rater Guidelines require elevated trust signals for sites selling ingestible products. Onyx has no named founder, no city/state, no phone number, no direct email — only a contact form. The "about" page (230 words) contains zero business information. Google's quality systems and AI answer engines both downrank anonymous health product brands. The $54.99 price point also demands trust credibility that the site cannot currently supply.
Fix: Add a named founder section to the About page, publish a city/state location in the footer, add a direct email address to the Contact page. Even first name + role + brief bio dramatically improves E-E-A-T signals.
Critical
Zero Blog Content — No Topical Authority, No Keyword Targeting Surface
The blog section exists (/blogs/news) and is indexed by Google — but contains zero posts. This wastes crawl budget on an empty page while simultaneously meaning the brand cannot rank for any of the ingredient-level, informational, or comparison queries that drive supplement purchases. Competitors like Gorilla Mind, Transparent Labs, and Legion have 50–150+ blog posts targeting these queries. The ingredient list (L-Citrulline, Alpha-GPC, Rhodiola, Huperzine A) is an untapped content goldmine that could rank for dozens of low-competition terms.
Fix: Publish a minimum of 4–5 blog posts targeting ingredient education keywords before or at product launch. Noindex /blogs/news until at least 3 posts are live. Priority topics provided in Section 10.
Critical
Product Title Tag Has Zero SEO Keyword Value
Current title: Golden Hour – Onyx Supplements. The word "pre-workout," "supplement," "preworkout," or any category term appears nowhere in the title. "Golden Hour" alone generates zero organic search demand. Google uses the title tag as its primary on-page ranking signal and as the blue link text in SERPs. This page cannot rank for any pre-workout category query, and even branded searches ("Golden Hour pre-workout") would benefit from the category term in the title.
Fix: Change to: Golden Hour Pre-Workout | 8g Citrulline, No Prop Blends — Onyx Supplements (58 chars). Implement in Shopify Admin → Products → Golden Hour → SEO.
