About TutorTime

Why families choose TutorTime

ACT test prep is an investment in the future of every high school student. From freshmen to seniors, we provide the highest quality preparation for this critical piece of the college admissions process that you will find in the industry, guaranteed.

Meetings are held remotely, or in person for local clients. We accommodate the learning styles, goals, and schedules of our students and their demanding academic lives.

The imperative but often overlooked concepts we hone in on, paired with passionate and experienced instruction, set our approach apart from competitors. And we have the results to back it: score increases of up to 12 points. Many of our clients have been admitted to their dream schools, and some have earned full scholarships based exclusively on their ACT score.

We also offer assistance with the overall college application and admissions process, and a referral program where you can earn unlimited free sessions by sending your friends our way.

My story

16 years of helping students get where they want to go.

I have been tutoring for 16 years. I began while completing my undergraduate degree in International Relations at the University of California, Santa Barbara and the University of Utah. In 2018, I started my own business, and after taking a few years off to raise my kids, I am back.

I am passionate, experienced, and knowledgeable, and I take a unique approach to tutoring, particularly ACT prep, that has produced results rarely seen from larger tutoring agencies or individual tutors. I have helped countless students gain admission to their dream schools, with many earning significant scholarships because of their test scores.

Over the years, it has been my pride to watch my clients complete their undergraduate degrees, graduate degrees, and even med and law school. I have even had the opportunity to follow some students into their career paths. I take genuine joy in seeing students build confidence, and in their excitement when they realize how much they have improved.

Contact me today. I can't wait to assist you or your student on the journey to future success.
