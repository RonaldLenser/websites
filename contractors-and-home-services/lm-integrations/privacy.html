Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

L&M Integrations ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it's used.

How We Use Information

What We Don't Do

  • We do not share your information with third parties for marketing

Third-Party Services

Contact form submissions are processed by Web3Forms and delivered to our email. Analytics are provided by GoatCounter. Each service processes only the data needed to perform its function.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry information as long as needed to serve your project and meet business record requirements. You can request deletion of your information at any time.
